Help

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Send a cab or TTD enquiry and our team will call you.

How do I book a bus ticket?

Choose your From and To cities and the date in the search card, then tap Search buses. Pick a bus, tap View seats, choose your seats and boarding point, and add passenger details to confirm.

Can I change the number of passengers after searching?

Yes. On the Bus tickets page, use the − and + buttons in the blue banner. The number of seats you can pick changes with it.

How does a cab enquiry work?

Fill in the Cab Enquiry card with pickup, drop, date, passengers, your name and mobile. There is no online payment. Our team calls you with available cars and fares, then confirms your driver by SMS.

Do you sell TTD darshan tickets?

Darshan, seva and accommodation tickets are issued only by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams. We help you with the enquiry and guide you through the process; our team calls to verify your details.

Where can I see my bookings?

Tap My bookings at the top. Bus bookings and enquiries made on this device are listed there, and enquiry status updates live as our team works on them.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Cancellation and rescheduling depend on the operator's policy for your bus or cab. Contact our help desk with your reference number and we will guide you.
