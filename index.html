Before you go

TTD darshan rules & tips

Tirumala has strict rules. Read these before you travel so nothing stops you at the queue.

Full name

Exactly as printed on the ID proof you will carry.

Age and gender

For every devotee in the group, including children.

Photo ID number

Aadhaar is preferred; passport or voter ID also work. Carry the original on the day.

Mobile number

Your ticket and slot details are sent to this number.

Men

Dhoti or pancha with a shirt, or kurta with pyjama.

Women

Saree, half-saree, or churidar with a dupatta.

Not allowed

Jeans, shorts, T-shirts, sleeveless tops and other western wear are not allowed for darshan.

Tip

Pack your traditional clothes in your day bag; you change at the fresh-up stop before darshan.

₹300 Special Entry Darshan

Issued only by TTD from its online quota, so slots depend on availability. Our team tells you before you pay whether your package includes a confirmed slot.

Children

Rules for children and infants change from time to time. Tell us their ages and we confirm what applies for your date.

Laddu prasadam

Laddu prasadam is given after darshan. Extra laddus can be bought at the TTD laddu counters in Tirumala.

Special sevas

Sevas are separate TTD bookings and are not part of the packages. Ask our team if you want one.

  1. Report on timeBe at the queue complex entry printed on your ticket at the reporting time. Your coordinator walks you there.
  2. Leave phones and camerasMobiles, cameras and electronics are not allowed inside the temple. Deposit them at the free TTD cloak rooms, or leave them on the bus.
  3. No photos insidePhotography is not allowed inside the main temple complex.
  4. Tonsure (mundan)Hair offering is done at Kalyanakatta. Tell your coordinator and we plan the time for it.
SRIVANI VIP (break) darshan

₹10,000 donation to the SRIVANI Trust plus a ₹500 ticket, per person. Booked on the official TTD website under Pilgrim Services → SRIVANI Donations.

  1. Log in to the official TTD website.
  2. Open Pilgrim Services → SRIVANI Donations → Continue to Donate.
  3. Enter ₹10,000 per person and choose Advance Booking.
  4. Add each pilgrim's name, age, gender and ID proof. Pick Break Darshan, then your date. Add a room if you need one.
  5. Check the summary and pay.
  6. Download the donation receipt and darshan ticket.
SRIVANI daily quota

TTD splits SRIVANI tickets into an advance online quota (about 3 months ahead), a current-booking counter at Tirupati Airport, and a share reserved for existing donors. For same-day tickets, booking opens at 9:00 AM, closes at 1:00 PM, and darshan is at 4:00 PM.

Usual monthly release order

TTD opens each month's quota about three months ahead, usually in this order:

  1. Arjitha seva lucky-dip registration
  2. Arjitha seva tickets, then virtual seva
  3. Angapradakshinam
  4. SRIVANI darshan
  5. Senior citizens & differently-abled quota
  6. ₹300 Special Entry Darshan
  7. Accommodation
Our help

Dates and quotas change every month. Send a TTD enquiry and our team tells you what is open for your dates and helps you book.

TTD rooms without online booking

If online rooms are sold out, register in person at the CRO office in Tirumala.

  • Go early, between 5:00 and 7:00 AM, for faster allotment; expect delays after 8:00 AM.
  • Register with Aadhaar and a mobile number.
  • Roughly 1,500–2,300 rooms are allotted offline each day.
  • Rooms are not given to single persons or unmarried couples.

Help

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Send a cab or TTD enquiry and our team will call you.

How do I book a bus ticket?

Choose your From and To cities and the date in the search card, then tap Search buses. Pick a bus, tap View seats, choose your seats and boarding point, and add passenger details to confirm.

Can I change the number of passengers after searching?

Yes. On the Bus tickets page, use the − and + buttons in the blue banner. The number of seats you can pick changes with it.

How does a cab enquiry work?

Fill in the Cab Enquiry card with pickup, drop, date, passengers, your name and mobile. There is no online payment. Our team calls you with available cars and fares, then confirms your driver by SMS.

Do you sell TTD darshan tickets?

Darshan, seva and accommodation tickets are issued only by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams. We help you with the enquiry and guide you through the process; our team calls to verify your details.

Where can I see my bookings?

Tap My bookings at the top. Bus bookings and enquiries made on this device are listed there, and enquiry status updates live as our team works on them.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Cancellation and rescheduling depend on the operator's policy for your bus or cab. Contact our help desk with your reference number and we will guide you.
